## Constitution & Engineering Standards

1. **Zero Assumed Success (ZAS)**: Verification through empirical stdout and test suites before sign-off.
2. **Fail-Closed Security**: Strict secrets isolation and credential containment.
3. **Acyclic Architecture**: Modular boundaries with zero circular dependencies or generic junk drawers.
